## Scenario B: cannot assist, including nobody available

Start again with the same uncertainty and the original baseline. Let the first contact say they cannot assist even if they already acknowledged. Do not pressure them to accept. Record their reason only if voluntarily offered and safe to retain.

Walk through the agreed escalation order on paper. Each subsequent contact needs its own permission, language comprehension, willingness and availability evidence; the first person's acceptance cannot cover another person. Ask the pair to explain the handoff, how they would know who has taken the action and what they would do if a response is absent. No reply stays unknown.

Then use an invented case in which every authorised contact explicitly cannot assist or is unavailable. The honest terminal outcome is **no contact can act**. If any availability is unknown, record that uncertainty. Ask the pair to explain the limit in their own words and choose a safe next step within their current arrangements. Stop the enrolment decision and revise the promise when nobody can act. Do not invent nearby assistance, automatically dispatch emergency services or mark an incident resolved after acknowledgement.
