## Gate before any future moving assessment

protocol and supervision before a future separately authorised simulator or
appropriately supervised closed-course session. Obtain the prerequisite
stationary comprehension evidence first. Keep #11's evidence/stop contract,
Robert's #23 policy, #4 and all applicable device/context gates explicit. The
author cannot satisfy independence by reviewing their own implementation.

The reviewer must prespecify and justify `B_attention`, its measures/units,
baseline comparison, uncertainty/missing-data handling, exposure/sample rationale
and stop criteria **before** seeing outcomes. Leave the limit unset until the
reviewer and evidence-contract owner provide it. No fixture time, task-completion
time, sample count or self-reported ease is a substitute attention threshold.
Missing limits, an unavailable assessor or unsupported conditions preclude a
moving-use pass. The offline evaluator remains stationary-only in every event.

Use simulator first where feasible. A closed course needs a specifically approved
supervision/safety protocol and qualified staff, never an ordinary public-road
trip. Never induce fatigue, sleepiness, robbery or an actual roadside emergency.
Keep existing assistance available. Stop immediately for app-induced distraction,
unsafe driving, discomfort, inability to supervise or a critical misunderstanding;
retain the failed/aborted trial. Do not increase prompts to create response labels.
