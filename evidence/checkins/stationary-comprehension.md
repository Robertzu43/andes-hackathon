# Blank stationary comprehension protocol and form · #24

**PREPARATORY — NOT EXECUTED.** No participant evidence has been collected by
this kit. All fields below are intentionally blank. This protocol is for a
future separately authorised study; it does not authorise recruitment, contact
or collection. Synthetic roleplay and software tests are different evidence.

## Scope and prerequisites

Use an ordinary, alert volunteer only after the study's consent, privacy,
retention, support and stop arrangements are approved. Sebastian coordinates;
the independent reviewer records a separate disposition. Keep #23/#4 and #11
limits open until their owners supply the required evidence and policy version.

Use a stationary room/desk or a securely parked truck away from an active road,
with no driving task and no emergency. Do not induce fatigue or simulate an
actual roadside emergency. Never ask a driver in motion to participate. Stop on
discomfort, distraction, unclear consent, unsafe setting or a request to stop.
Real help remains available outside the study and cannot depend on completion.

The target group is independent freight owner-operators with usually one or two
trucks. Record only the country, locale and device conditions actually studied;
include Brazil/PT-BR in future coverage decisions. Do not infer region-wide
support from a local sample. No participant-facing ES/PT-BR script is provided
here. Prepare and review the applicable language script separately before use,
mark it untested until comprehension is observed, and version it with the task.

## Procedure for a future authorised session

1. Freeze the candidate build/contract SHA, approved script, stationary baseline
   and scenario order before observing outcomes. Record the #23 dependency
   status. Keep the baseline condition safe and comparable; do not invent timing
   or notification behaviour while that policy is unresolved.
2. Demonstrate neutral, stationary vignettes for OK, rest and help; no reply;
   unavailable permission; stale/unknown motion or road; a traffic-light stop;
   a planned stop before and after parking; and a contradictory observation.
   Use offline examples only. No provider message or alarm is sent.
3. Ask for unaided explanation of what the system knows, what remains unknown,
   when interaction is permitted and what each response means. Observe the
   person's task choices before giving feedback. Record errors, omissions,
   assistance, task time and uncertainty against the frozen rubric.
4. Compare to the prespecified baseline. Keep self-reported ease in a separate
   column from observed comprehension/task performance. Preserve the first
   unaided answer and every failed attempt; a coached retry is a separate trial.
5. Stop/revise on any critical misunderstanding that could cause distraction,
   delay help or create false reassurance. Missing observations stay missing.
   Obtain an independent pass/revise/stop/inconclusive disposition for this
   stationary scope only. No response is never labelled fatigue or robbery.
