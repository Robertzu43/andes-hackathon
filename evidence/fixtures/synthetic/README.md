# SYNTHETIC fixture: invented data

Every person, trip, time and record in this folder is invented for tests. Nothing here came from a real driver, contact or police record.

- Pair SYN-A (BR, pt-BR) carries a loaded interurban haul. The driver reports tiredness, then rests longer than planned without signal. Unknown contact status has its own row, separate from the contact's explicitly attributed fear of robbery in interview source SYN-A-W-S2. The unanswered calls do not create that suspicion. The driver reports no robbery; this denial is also an account. The pair's ordinary trip is left out of the export because its redaction review is unfinished.
- Pair SYN-B (CO, es) carries an attempted robbery that the driver reports. Wholly invented source SYN-B-W-S4 describes an officer's review of invented roadside video showing two people blocking this truck and trying to force its cab door before it escapes. Those independent observations support the specific attempt in SYN-B-W-E04. Its observed time is the invented video time of the attempt, not a complaint filing time. A report that only repeated the driver's allegation would remain an attributed self-report. No video, officer, incident or police record exists. The pair has no ordinary trip, so `check` warns.
- Negative tests replace the independent observations with a mere complaint or unknown basis, and try to turn unanswered call metadata into fatigue or robbery suspicions. Each must be refused. Positive tests preserve unknown contact status and allow an explicitly attributed self-report without upgrading it.
- `expected-export.json` is the exact output of `export` for this folder. The tests compare against it and against hand-calculated values.
