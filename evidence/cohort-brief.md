## What the reconstruction tests

The #8 claim is that in the first cohort there is avoidable delay between an unconfirmed or unsafe trip status and a useful action by a trusted contact. The reconstruction looks for that window in real freight trips, starting from original accounts and records.

It also gathers evidence on the founder's positioning hypothesis: that independent owner-operators with one or two trucks are poorly served by products built for large fleets. Treat it as a hypothesis. Record what drivers use today, who set it up and what it costs. Five pairs cannot support a prevalence figure, a market share or a willingness to pay.

## Who can take part

- Independent freight truck drivers (camioneros, caminhoneiros) and owner-operators in any Latin American country, including Brazil, who own or operate one or two trucks. No large-fleet employer is required, and Peru is not required.
- Each driver takes part with one trusted contact who usually follows their trips: family, a fellow driver, someone at the freight company or a friend.
- Local, interurban and cross-border freight trips all count.
- Nobody needs to have had an emergency, a robbery or a fatigue incident. Across the cohort, aim to cover both a security concern and tiredness or a rest problem; ordinary trips are the counterexamples.
- Adults who can give informed consent in Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese. Another language needs translated materials first, and its use is recorded.

Never interview anyone who is driving, and never recruit someone during an unresolved emergency. Nobody is asked to drive tired, stay awake, stage a robbery or create an emergency for this study.

## Sample

Up to five pairs. Each reconstructs one recent worrying freight trip and one ordinary freight trip; an ordinary trip with a harmless late reply is especially useful. Five pairs are exploratory. They cannot show how common a problem is, what the regional demand is or whether ANDES works.

Record how each pair was found, for example a drivers' WhatsApp group, a truck stop or a personal contact. The route into the sample shapes who is in it.

## Data handling (decide before recruitment)

- Interview recordings, transcripts, screenshots, exports and police or insurer documents stay in private storage outside Git. Working spreadsheets go in `evidence/private/<cohort>/`, which Git ignores.
- Use codes only. The key linking codes to people stays offline with the consent forms.
- Unset: storage location, who has access, retention period and deletion process. Do not recruit until these are decided and written into the consent form.
- Country requirements for consent and personal data have not been reviewed. This brief makes no compliance claim for any country.
- Admitting to dozing at the wheel, describing cargo or reporting a robbery can carry legal, insurance or work consequences for a driver. The consent form must say who can see what, and interviewers promise nothing beyond it.
