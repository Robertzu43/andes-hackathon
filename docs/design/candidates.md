# Seven grounded operational worlds

Category rut excluded: enterprise fleet dashboard with a dominant live-map, vehicle counters and green safety indicators. Predictable opposite excluded: black surveillance console with alarm-red everything. ANDES-name literal mountains are not the organising metaphor.

1. Freight journey slip. A compact truck-and-trip identity followed by an ordered source-timestamp record and action area; the useful organisation of a consignment slip, without faux paper texture. Family: operational documents.
2. Road direction signs. High-legibility destination blocks, decisive arrows and explicit upcoming action; generous neutral field and deep directional colour. Family: wayfinding/signage.
3. Radio check conversation. Paired driver/contact turns with attributed speaker, event time and unanswered state; ordinary familiar conversation affordances without chat pretending to be delivery. Family: communications interfaces.
4. Cab control panel. Fixed, tactile-sized labelled controls beneath independent instrument readouts for response/location/alert state; no fake gauges or animated needles. Family: instruments/controls.
5. Relay handoff board. Clear named responsibility, available/unknown contact capacity and step-by-step handoff, with open unresolved item visible. Family: coordination boards.
6. Departure timetable. A short tabular route/time/status structure, late or unavailable facts visually distinguished through text and age, followed by explicit next actions. Family: transport information systems.
7. Journey checkpoints. A compact linear sequence of confirmed trip steps with separate side-by-side contact permissions; pending facts have open markers, never fabricated completion. Family: process notation.

All seven preserve task affordances, large controls, separate fact sources and simple localisable states. No actual fleet size, emergency service or safety efficacy is implied. This list is frozen before the concept seed runs. The selected system must serve OPERATE mode; unfamiliar catalogue forms lose if they obscure task/state or require decorative complexity.
