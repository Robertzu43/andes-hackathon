# ANDES identity

Product: phone-based trip and contact coordination for LATAM freight owner-operators. Idea: a useful response with an explicit source and time. Selected world: radio check conversation, derived from driver/contact exchanges; this is a fact record and action flow, not a chat bot.

Five answers: (1) the driver prepares and confirms a trip, shares scoped information and reports intent; accepted contacts can act. (2) The identity communicates clear mutual contact. (3) Reject fleet live-map dashboards and surveillance alarm consoles; use short attributed updates with separate factual readouts. (4) Name/mark must work at16px, mobile header, app-icon and neutral/dim backgrounds. (5) Avoid a medical pulse, shield guarantee, police emblem or decorative mountain implying proven protection.

Use a simple exact vector of two offset open brackets facing a central space, plus ANDES set in a real UI sans. Brackets represent two parties with a bounded shared view; no badge implying certification. Typeface: platform system-ui sans for task text and semibold wordmark, as explicitly permitted by product register; no font download, redistribution or display/body costume pairing. Tabular numerals only for times. Single family, regular/medium/semibold/bold. Platform font is deliberate for weak-network iPhone use. Foundation must inspect actual16px mark and deliver favicon16/32/48, app512, Apple180, and1200x630 generic share-card without route/participant data.

Physical scene: a parked driver reads a phone in strong daylight and a trusted contact glances at it indoors. Start with a light high-contrast neutral surface and ordinary familiar controls; add a dim theme only with matching contrast evidence, never infer it is safe to use while driving.

Colour strategy: restrained cool tinted neutrals. Ink #173139; canvas #F2F6F6; surface #FCFDFD; secondary #4D6268; primary #166675 with #F9FCFC text; warning #805312; danger #A93232; divider #CAD6D8. Translate the exact measured colours into OKLCH tokens. Accent allowance: primary enabled task action, active navigation/selection, visible focus ring and tiny wordmark accent only. Semantic warning/danger colours are separate and always carry text; red means help request/error only, never a proven robbery. No decorative colour rails, green wellbeing lamps or decorative gradients. Contrast measurements are in contrast.json; runtime measurement remains required.

Rejected alternatives: map-first hides uncertainty and contacts; alarm-console overstates monitoring; chat-bot bubbles imply a conversational capability absent from backend. Product primitives stay familiar and accessible. Mark imagery is code-authored vector geometry, not generated text or a stock emblem. Brand validation is pending the actual foundation build and independent review.
