# GOAT issue ownership

One accountable owner per issue. Section owners coordinate work across both contributors. All 44 existing issues remain open and keep their original requirements, blockers and acceptance gates.

## Division of work

- **Sebastian (`sebasbimbi`):** driver/customer evidence, provider-account access, mobile and contact UX, phone capability/capture, independent observation, consent and pilot/release decisions.
- **Robert (`Robertzu43`):** architecture, server state and event contracts, durable jobs, channel integrations, bounded policy logic and reproducible evaluation/analysis.

This allocation is inferred from available work and access, not a claim that either person is limited to one stack. Robert’s portfolio also contains frontend work. A named owner coordinates any required independent review and cannot sign off their own work as independent.

- Sebastian starts with [#8](https://github.com/Bimbi-Dev/andes-hackathon/issues/8) and owns the #9–#11 capability, provider and evidence gates.

## Geographic scope

ANDES serves independent freight truck drivers and owner-operators across Latin America, including Brazil, typically with one or two trucks. Robbery during freight transport and fatigue/sleepiness are the core problems. The individual driver is the primary customer; company fleet administration is not required. The existing 20/24 assignment split applies to the regional requirements in the plan and issues. Sebastian owns country/cohort evidence, localised UX and contact comprehension; Robert owns configurable country/language contracts, integrations and reproducible country-aware evaluation. A local pilot limits the claims it supports, not the regional product vision.
